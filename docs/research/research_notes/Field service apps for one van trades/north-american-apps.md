# North American field service apps: customer-facing experience and the awkward moments of a visit (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, simPRO, Workiz, FieldPulse)

Researched 10 October 2026. Currency of each claim is noted inline where the source carries a date; vendor help-centre pages are undated unless stated and should be read as "current as of fetch on 10 Oct 2026". Several primary pages returned 403/404 to automated fetch (Jobber product-update post on live tracking links, Jobber developer webhook docs, Housecall Pro employee-GPS article, Workiz mobile help article, simPRO Mobile 13.7 release notes, simPRO Notifications page); for those I relied on search-engine excerpts of the same pages and say so.

A note on scope: ServiceM8's "Track My Arrival" is the closest structural analogue to TurnUp (an SMS link that opens a live map, live only while the tech is "Navigating") and appeared repeatedly in results — [ServiceM8 help](https://support.servicem8.com/hc/en-us/articles/201190600-What-is-Track-My-Arrival). It is Australian, not North American, so it is mentioned only as a comparator.

## Key question 1: Customer-facing tracking and communication, app by app

### Takeaway
None of the six ships a native, always-on "live dot" for the customer out of the box. Jobber and Housecall Pro only produce a live-map link when the account buys Force Fleet Tracking hardware (US-only for Jobber); ServiceTitan has a native phone-GPS tracking page gated behind address verification; FieldPulse has a native one-hour phone-GPS link that must be sent by hand; Workiz describes an ETA-plus-route page via a third-party listing only; simPRO has SMS/email notifications and mobile status triggers but nothing I could find that shows the customer a map or ETA. Every app's "on my way" message is a one-shot text, not a persistent state page.

### Cited Findings

**Jobber**
- Native "On My Way" text: opened from a visit in the Schedule tab; tech picks the contact (defaults to primary), picks minutes away from **5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 45 or 60**, taps Send Text; the chosen value becomes the default next time. The callback number is set automatically (tech's profile number if present, else office number) and cannot be chosen at send time; the send screen previews the exact message. Available "on select plans" in the US, Canada and Great Britain. The help article does not say whether a link is included or whether a second text can be sent for the same visit — [Jobber Help: On my way texts](https://help.getjobber.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021265374)
- Jobber's own feature page frames the same button for lateness: "Let customers know you're on the way (or running late) with a quick text. Just select your ETA and hit send" — [Jobber feature page](https://www.getjobber.com/features/simplify-customer-communications/)
- 2023 product update: on-my-way text added with the service pro's phone number so "customers can directly contact their assigned service pro"; Connect and Grow plans, US/Canada/UK — [Jobber product update, 2023](https://productupdates.getjobber.com/news/15502-send-on-my-way-text)
- Live tracking link (August 2025 product update, page returned 403 on fetch; details from search excerpt): when the tech taps On My Way the text can include a live tracking link "powered by Force Fleet Tracking"; the link is unique per customer and "automatically expires once the visit is complete or after the scheduled end time"; **limited to US accounts on Connect or higher using the Force Fleet Tracking integration** — [Jobber product update, Aug 2025](https://productupdates.getjobber.com/102703-force-fleet-tracking-integration-enhances-on-my-way-texts-with-live-tracking-links)
- Arrival windows: default set in Work Settings; options None (default), 15 min, 30 min, 1 h, 2 h, 3 h, 4 h; two styles, "Add window after start time" (9 AM start → window opens 9 AM) or "Center window on start time" (2 h window, 9 AM start → 8–10 AM). Shown to clients in the client hub appointments, booking confirmations (email and text) and visit reminders (email and text) via `{{ARRIVAL_WINDOW}}` template variable. **Not mentioned in the on-my-way text.** The internal schedule shows only the start time — [Jobber Help: Arrival windows](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/arrival-windows/); plan availability "Connect and Grow" per [product update](https://productupdates.getjobber.com/16187-consistently-meet-client-expectations-with-arrival-windows)
- Known customer-side problem: a Jobber community user reports reminders put the start time first and the arrival window lower in the message, so customers "often miss the window" — [Jobber Community](https://community.getjobber.com/discussions/job-details-scheduling/client-reminders/8590)
- Two-way texting is a Grow-plan feature (US/UK/Canada); with it, on-my-way messages go from a dedicated business number and client replies land in Jobber's message centre; without it the text carries the tech's or office's callback number — [Jobber Help: two-way messaging](https://help.getjobber.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051087154); confirmed on [pricing page](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) ("Connect with customers through two-way SMS" under Grow)
- Customer appointment confirmation by reply ("Yes to confirm / No to reschedule") is a **feature request**, not shipped — [Jobber Community](https://community.getjobber.com/discussions/customer-management-and-self-serve/customer-appointment-confirmations/7337)
- Tipping: toggle "Accept tips from clients" in Invoice preferences enables tips in client hub and the app; presets 10/15/20% or custom; works with Jobber Payments and Tap to Pay; not available on offline payments — [Jobber Help: tip collection](https://help.getjobber.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410197509783). Launch data (2022): ~24% of homeowners opted to tip, nearly $3M in tips in five months — [Total Landscape Care, 2022](https://www.totallandscapecare.com/technology/article/15292973/jobber-announces-new-tipping-and-referral-feature)
- Automated Google review requests sit in the Marketing Suite add-on (reported as $99/mo, included in Plus) and fire on job close, visit completion or final invoice paid — third-party blog, treat price as secondhand: [sagnikbhattacharya.com](https://sagnikbhattacharya.com/blog/ai-review-follow-ups-trades). Jobber's pricing page lists the Marketing Suite at $79/mo per [FieldProxy, verified June 2026](https://www.fieldproxy.ai/fsm-software-pricing/jobber) — the two sources conflict.

**Housecall Pro**
- Automated notification types: Job/Estimate Scheduled (text + email; re-sent if time or assigned employee changes), On My Way (**text only**, fired when the tech taps the button), Job/Estimate Finished (text only), Review Request (email + text, requires Reviews app), marketing campaigns. Landlines get no texts; customers with only an email get email — [HCP Help: Customer notifications overview](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/357613-customer-notifications-overview)
- Text templates are editable (Settings → Notifications → Customer tab → pencil icon) only "if you have a plan that includes the Custom text notifications feature"; templates use smart variables, apply to every customer, and can include the photo of the employee going to the job — [HCP Help: customize SMS](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/12417565-customize-sms-notifications); [HCP Help: notifications in settings](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/14171435-customer-notifications-in-settings)
- Without Vehicle GPS Tracking, the On My Way text is a plain notification. With it ($20/vehicle/month, no contract, Force Fleet Tracking hardware), the text includes a link to the driver's location plus vehicle, licence plate, assigned employees' names and photos, "job stats", schedule and a contact option. **Link refreshes every 10 seconds and expires when the tech presses Start, Finish or Invoice/Pay, or after 2 hours, whichever first.** Trucks without a Force device send links without vehicle data. Sharing can be switched off via the app card's "Share location with customer" toggle. HCP recommends wording the text "track your tech here:" — [HCP Help: Vehicle GPS tracking overview](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/6060351-vehicle-gps-tracking-overview)
- The customer "Job Preview" page (linked from the on-my-way text, Force-only) shows service address, job status, scheduled time, a contact card (text/call/email) and employee photos or initials; showing line items "is no longer supported" — [HCP Help: Job preview for customers](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/1344386-job-preview-for-customers)
- Review requests: auto-sent when job is finished (or after payment); Gmail addresses routed to Google by default, others split between Facebook and the business website; follow-up reminders available — [HCP resource](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/how-to-ask-for-google-reviews/)
- Tipping exists: credit-card tips sync to QuickBooks as a "Housecall Pro Tips" income account — [HCP Help](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/6341938-managing-credit-card-tips-in-quickbooks-online); "digital tipping" listed among features in the Sept 2025 voice-invoicing launch — [Contracting Business, Sep 2025](https://contractingbusiness.com/industry-news/press-release/55316860/housecall-pro-brings-ai-powered-voice-activated-invoicing-to-the-trades)

**ServiceTitan**
- Dispatch notifications (text and email set up separately) fire automatically when the first technician is dispatched; later techs to the same site that day do not re-trigger unless everyone has left and someone is re-dispatched. A setting "Limit dispatch notifications when multiple techs are dispatched" caps it at one per day. Text requires a default outbound SMS number; arrival tracking requires native GPS on each tech's device and the "Tech Tracking URL" token added to the template. **If the service address is verified, the customer's tracking screen shows distance, ETA and an interactive map; if not verified, it shows only the technician's name and the destination address.** Technician photos are shown; the technician's rating is shown only if 4 stars or above. If auto-arrive is on, the arrival appears on the tracking screen — [ServiceTitan Help: dispatch notifications](https://help.servicetitan.com/docs/enable-text-and-email-dispatch-notifications.md)
- Marketing copy: "show customers where their technician's truck is on a map, and definitively know when they arrive" — [ServiceTitan CX page](https://www.servicetitan.com/customer-experience-software); bios/photos and real-time arrival tracking date from a 2017 release — [ServiceTitan press, 2017](https://servicetitan.com/press/servicetitan-introduces-new-customer-communications)
- ServiceTitan's own playbook: a bio "helps the customer connect with the tech before arrival" — [ServiceTitan dispatch notifications guide](https://www.servicetitan.com/guides/contractor-playbook/dispatch-notifications); third-party pulseM automates bio sending — [pulseM](https://ask.pulsem.me/en/knowledge/dispatch-automation)

**simPRO**
- SMS from Schedule View on a job, quote or activity; notifications can be triggered "on a schedule or when a workflow condition is met" with merged job data; SMS plan must be registered and paid for; SMS not available in Canada; in US/AU registered sender IDs required and **return (two-way) messaging unavailable in US and AU** — [simPRO help: Setup SMS](https://helpguide.simprogroup.com/Content/Service-and-Enterprise/Setup-simPRO-SMS.htm); [simPRO help: Notifications (last updated 15 Dec 2025)](https://helpguide.simprogroup.com/Content/Service-and-Enterprise/Notifications.htm) (article body did not render on fetch; details from search excerpts); [simPRO SMS marketplace listing](https://marketplace.simprogroup.com/apps/simpro-sms-messaging)
- Simpro Mobile 13.4 separated travel from work start; 13.7 added "Travel Stopped" and "Travel Back" mobile triggers under Setup → Mobile → Statuses → Mobile Triggers; geostamps record where a status change happened — [Simpro Mobile 13.4 release](https://helpguide.simprogroup.com/Content/Releases/simpro-mobile-13.4.htm); [Simpro Mobile 13.7 release](https://helpguide.simprogroup.com/Content/Releases/simpro-mobile-13.7.htm) (403 on fetch; from search excerpt); [Mobile Status Log Report, updated 8 Dec 2025](https://helpguide.simprogroup.com/Content/Service-and-Enterprise/Mobile-Status-Log-Report.htm)
- simPRO markets customer portals and fleet tracking (simTRAC) but I found nothing showing a customer a technician's position or ETA — [simPRO portals](https://www.simprogroup.com/uk/features/portals); [simPRO fleet tracking](https://www.simprogroup.com/au/features/fleet-tracking)

**Workiz**
- Only source describing the customer view is a third-party marketplace listing: "Clients receive a text with a link to view the tech's photo, bio, and ETA via the client portal", clients "can follow the technician's route and arrival time in real time", "ETAs are calculated using GPS and traffic data", office gets alerts when techs send updates or arrive — [SoftwareOne marketplace listing for Workiz On My Way](https://marketplace.platform.softwareone.com/product/on-my-way/PCP-1479-8041). Not confirmed against Workiz's own help centre.
- Workiz's mobile help article (403 on fetch; search excerpt): from the job screen "Select ETA to let your client know you're on your way or that you'll be a few minutes late" — [Workiz Help: Managing jobs on mobile](https://help.workiz.com/en/articles/4812389-managing-jobs-on-mobile)
- Workiz's job-status marketing page mentions customisable appointment reminders and On My Way texts — [Workiz job status page](https://workiz.com/features/field-service-job-management/job-status/)

**FieldPulse**
- "Trip Tracking SMS": Actions → Send Customer Communication → SMS → Send Trip Tracking SMS to Customer. Customer gets a text with a link to a live map of the tech's real-time location; tech can adjust the time estimate before sending; **link stays active for one hour after sending**; the first send writes the default message which is reused later; **must be sent manually, cannot be tied to automatic triggers**; can go from the FieldPulse number or the tech's own phone (then it comes from the tech's personal number); requires location services on. Profile-photo SMS and file attachments also available — [FieldPulse Help: customer communications mobile app](https://help.fieldpulse.com/en/articles/2517252-customer-communications-mobile-app)
- Fleet Tracking add-on: vehicles update every minute, geofence enter/exit alerts, after-hours movement alerts; no customer-facing feature described on that page — [FieldPulse fleet management](https://www.fieldpulse.com/features/fleet-management)

### Inferences
- The industry default for "on my way" is **a technician-chosen number of minutes** (Jobber's 5–60 picker, FieldPulse's adjustable estimate, Workiz's "few minutes late"), not a computed ETA. Computed ETAs appear only when a GPS feed exists (ServiceTitan's verified-address tracking screen, Force-powered HCP links, Workiz's claimed GPS+traffic ETA).
- Live-map links are short-lived by design everywhere: HCP 2 h or until Start, Jobber until visit complete/scheduled end, FieldPulse 1 h, ServiceM8 only while "Navigating". A TurnUp page that persists through On site → Paused → Job done is a different model from all of them; none of the six keeps the customer page alive once the tech arrives, which is exactly where TurnUp's Paused/Job done stages live.
- Arrival windows (Jobber) and on-my-way texts are separate systems that do not reference each other; the window appears in reminders and the hub, the ETA in minutes appears in the text. Nobody reconciles the two for the customer.

### Gaps
- Exact default wording of Jobber's and Housecall Pro's on-my-way texts: neither help article quotes it (both show it only in the editor/preview).
- Whether Jobber lets a tech send a second on-my-way text for the same visit (running later) — help article silent.
- Whether Workiz's client ETA page shows a live map natively or only an ETA figure; Workiz's own help article could not be fetched.
- Any customer-facing ETA/location feature in simPRO: none found; may not exist.
- Housecall Pro's standard (non-Force) on-my-way text: no evidence it contains any link at all.

## Key question 2: Technician-side GPS, geofence arrival, auto clock-in, offline

### Takeaway
ServiceTitan is the only one with documented, default-on geofence auto-arrival (125 m, address must be verified). Jobber has geofenced "location timers" in two modes (automatic or push-prompt) but they switch an already-running timer rather than start the day, are Grow-plan only, and have reliability complaints. Housecall Pro's phone GPS is for the dispatcher map only, with denser sampling once a job is Started. Offline support is weak across the board.

### Cited Findings
- **ServiceTitan Arrive by GPS**: dispatched techs with GPS-enabled devices are automatically marked Arrived within **125 metres** of the job's service location; the address must be validated by the office or lat/long entered manually; feature enabled by default; GPS accurate 3–10 m, cellular fallback ~500 m; techs told to set location permission to "Always"; removing a device from the GPS provider list disables all GPS features including real-time tracking; techs can tap Arrive manually — [ServiceTitan Help: Arrive by GPS](https://help.servicetitan.com/docs/arrive-by-gps-for-servicetitan-mobile); [enable/disable](https://help.servicetitan.com/docs/enable-or-disable-arrive-by-gps-for-technicians-in-fma); [troubleshooting](https://help.servicetitan.com/docs/not-auto-arriving-to-jobs-in-servicetitan-mobile). I found no setting to change the 125 m radius.
- **Jobber location timers**: geofences around client properties; Automatic mode starts/stops the timer on arrival/departure; Reminder mode sends a push "when the service pro is near a client's property" and they start/stop with a tap; **Grow plan** — [Jobber Help: timers and timesheets](https://help.getjobber.com/hc/en-us/articles/115009560687); [Jobber product update](https://productupdates.getjobber.com/32549-maintain-accurate-and-up-to-date-timesheets-with-location-timers); [Jobber feature page](https://getjobber.com/features/automatic-time-tracking-software/)
- Jobber Support reply in community (not official docs): the general timesheet timer must already be running; location timers "do not start a workday on their own, they only switch the timer from general time to job time once a technician arrives" — [Jobber Community](https://community.getjobber.com/discussions/job-details-scheduling/location-timers-still-not-working/8186). Same thread: a user's auto timers "worked for about a couple of weeks and now have not worked for 2 months" despite reinstalling.
- Jobber offline: one help page says geofenced timers won't work without internet; a newer article says timers work in all network conditions including offline — conflict noted in [search summary of Jobber Help](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/timesheets/). Third-party reviewers say Jobber has "no offline mode" — [Tofu](https://tofu.com/blog/jobber-vs-housecall-pro); a competitor table says techs can view cached visits but not edit — [Service Fusion blog (competitor)](https://www.servicefusion.com/blog/introducing-offline-mode)
- **Housecall Pro employee GPS** (help article 404 on direct fetch; from search excerpt): phone-based "GPS by job" for the assigned tech; employees appear on the map only during set business hours; "when a job is marked as started, Housecall Pro will take a more frequent and accurate location assessment" — [HCP Help: GPS how to track your employees](https://help.housecallpro.com/articles/362425-gps-how-to-track-your-employees). Employee GPS is included from the Essentials plan — [HCP pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/). No geofence auto-arrival or auto clock-in documented.
- HCP Force geofences alert when a vehicle leaves after hours, arrives at the office or reaches a job site; admins see vehicles as Offline / Parked / En Route — [HCP Help: Vehicle GPS](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/6060351-vehicle-gps-tracking-overview)
- HCP offline: Mobile FAQ says stored job data is viewable without signal from app versions 2024.8.1 (Android) / 2024.8.20 (iOS), limited to the jobs area — [HCP Mobile FAQ](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/4747652-housecall-pro-mobile-faq)
- **simPRO**: travel started/stopped/back as independent mobile statuses with geostamps (see Q1) — [Simpro Mobile 13.4](https://helpguide.simprogroup.com/Content/Releases/simpro-mobile-13.4.htm); simTRAC is a separate GPS fleet product — [GetApp simTRAC](https://www.getapp.com/sales-software/a/simtrac/)
- **FieldPulse**: fleet add-on updates every minute with geofence alerts; no auto clock-in described — [FieldPulse fleet](https://www.fieldpulse.com/features/fleet-management). Reviewers complain the app lacks offline access to the price book and has sync problems; FieldPulse replied offline improvements are "in the works" — [Capterra FieldPulse reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/153475/FieldPulse/reviews?page=2)
- **Workiz**: a competitor comparison says Workiz has phone-based location tracking and vehicle GPS needs a third party — [Rework (competitor-adjacent)](https://resources.rework.com/tools/field-service/best-workiz-alternatives); Workiz's own GPS page — [Workiz GPS](https://workiz.com/industries/hvac/hvac-gps-tracking-software). No offline documentation found.

### Inferences
- "It looks like you arrived" is implemented today only as Jobber's Reminder-mode push and ServiceTitan's silent auto-arrive; nobody documents a *reconstruction* flow (back-filling a missed arrival from the GPS trail). That is open space for TurnUp.
- The 125 m geofence and verified-address prerequisite in ServiceTitan are a useful calibration: auto-arrival is only trusted when the destination coordinates are known to be right.

### Gaps
- Time-reconstruction / retroactive arrival editing: not documented by any vendor I could reach.
- Workiz and simPRO offline behaviour: no sources.
- Dispatch push-notification mechanics (tech-side) for Workiz, FieldPulse, simPRO: not found beyond generic marketing.

## Key question 3: Exceptions — late tech, no-show, customer not home, overrun, cancellation enforcement

### Takeaway
No vendor documents a dedicated "customer not home" or "tech no-show" status, an automated late-arrival message, or automated cancellation-fee enforcement. Lateness is handled by re-sending the same on-my-way/ETA text; fees are handled by manual invoicing workarounds; the cancel action itself has hard edges (ServiceTitan blocks cancellation when payments exist).

### Cited Findings
- Jobber: the on-my-way button doubles as the running-late tool ("on the way (or running late)… select your ETA") — [Jobber feature page](https://www.getjobber.com/features/simplify-customer-communications/)
- Jobber: deposits can be taken on quotes but not on regular jobs or online bookings; the community workaround for no-shows is to store a card, state the policy in the service agreement, replace the job's line items with one "cancellation fee" item, create and send an invoice, then collect — a manual process — [Jobber Community (c. 2025)](https://community.getjobber.com/discussions/invoicing-getting-paid/deposit-for-cancellations/2521)
- Jobber: no documented no-show status; mass weather reschedules are handled by texting clients, "depending on your plan" — [Jobber Community](https://community.getjobber.com/discussions/job-details-scheduling/rescheduling-multiple-clients-at-once/1284)
- Workiz: ETA button covers "you'll be a few minutes late" — [Workiz Help (excerpt)](https://help.workiz.com/en/articles/4812389-managing-jobs-on-mobile)
- Housecall Pro: its advice on no-shows is to "email the customer, waive the fee once, and invite them to reschedule" and to let customers apply a cancellation fee or lost deposit to a future job — guidance, not a product feature — [HCP resource: last-minute cancellations](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/last-minute-cancellations/). Cancelling a Job Inbox lead has "no repercussions" for the pro — [HCP Help](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/5709276-how-to-cancel-a-job-received-through-job-inbox)
- Housecall Pro: the Job Scheduled notification is automatically re-sent if the time or assigned employee changes (the only automated "reschedule" message found) — [HCP Help](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/357613-customer-notifications-overview)
- ServiceTitan: "Jobs cannot be canceled when invoices contain active payments or tasks" (error: "Job cannot be canceled while invoice contains any payments"); workaround is to complete rather than cancel; jobs cannot be deleted — [ServiceTitan Help: cancel a job](https://help.servicetitan.com/how-to/cancel-job). Cancel reasons live under Settings → Operations → Cancel and Hold — third-party guide [Blue Corona](https://bluecorona.helpjuice.com/servicetitan/jobs-cancelled)
- ServiceTitan: a same-day cancellation fee is charged by cancelling the job and creating an adjustment invoice with a "cancellation fee" service item — user advice in [ServiceTitan Community, May 2023](https://community.servicetitan.com/t5/Accounting/Charge-Same-Day-Cancellation-Fee/m-p/31799)
- ServiceTitan practitioner practice: a shop uses a two-hour arrival window, set times for first calls, and tells customers "the windows are approximate and will be updated if the tech runs late" — [ServiceTitan Community](https://community.servicetitan.com/t5/Call-Booking/How-are-you-scheduling-calls/m-p/11796)
- Oracle Field Service (enterprise, for contrast) lets operators **lock a communicated ETA** so later re-sequencing doesn't silently change what the customer was told — [Oracle docs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/field-service/fawmt/c-wmt-overview-use-communicated-windows.html)

### Inferences
- Because every product treats lateness as "send the on-my-way text again with a bigger number", the customer sees two contradictory ETAs with no explanation. A tracking page that shows the *revised* promise and marks it as revised would be a visible improvement.
- Evidence for a no-show dispute (timestamps, GPS, photo of the door) is not productised by any of the six; the raw material exists (simPRO geostamps, ServiceTitan auto-arrive, HCP "Started" sampling) but is not surfaced to the customer.

### Gaps
- No vendor documentation found for a "customer not home" status, automatic no-show fee, or late-arrival auto-text, in any of the six. Absence of evidence after targeted searches, not confirmed absence.
- simPRO and FieldPulse cancellation mechanics: not found.

## Key question 4: Jobber's and Housecall Pro's live-map "on my way" in depth — what the customer can and cannot see, privacy

### Takeaway
Both are **vehicle-tracker** features from the same partner (Force Fleet Tracking), not phone GPS: the dot is the van, not the person, and it exists only if the business pays per vehicle. HCP documents the richer page (10-second refresh, plate, photos, contact card, 2 h/Start expiry, admin toggle to stop sharing); Jobber's is US-only and expires at visit completion or scheduled end. Neither shows the customer other stops, queue position or a route.

### Cited Findings
- Housecall Pro: link shows driver location, vehicle and licence number, assigned employees' names and photos (initials if no photo), job stats, schedule, contact option; refresh every 10 s; expires on Start/Finish/Invoice-Pay or 2 h; "Share location with customer" toggle; $20/vehicle/month; trucks with no device → link without vehicle data — [HCP Help: Vehicle GPS](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/6060351-vehicle-gps-tracking-overview); Job Preview page content and "line items no longer supported" — [HCP Help: Job preview](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/1344386-job-preview-for-customers); employee photo can be embedded in the on-my-way text on any plan with custom text — [HCP Help: customize SMS](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/12417565-customize-sms-notifications)
- Housecall Pro marketing describes the link as showing GPS location, vehicle make and plate — [HCP vehicle GPS feature page](https://www.housecallpro.com/features/vehicle-gps-tracking/); partner page — [Force Fleet × HCP](https://forcefleet.com/integrations/housecallpro/)
- Jobber: unique-per-customer link, expires at visit completion or scheduled end time, **US accounts, Connect or higher, Force Fleet integration required** (Aug 2025) — [Jobber product update](https://productupdates.getjobber.com/102703-force-fleet-tracking-integration-enhances-on-my-way-texts-with-live-tracking-links) (403 on fetch; search excerpt)
- Jobber privacy mechanics for the plain text: the callback number is the tech's profile number by default; to stop it being exposed, remove it from the profile (or store it in a custom field, which is never used as callback); a number used for Jobber Payments 2FA is never exposed — [Jobber Help](https://help.getjobber.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021265374)
- ServiceTitan contrast: native phone GPS, map + distance + ETA only when the address is verified; otherwise name + destination only — [ServiceTitan Help](https://help.servicetitan.com/docs/enable-text-and-email-dispatch-notifications.md)
- ServiceM8 contrast: live map available only while the tech is in "Navigating" state, deactivates shortly after — [ServiceM8 Help](https://support.servicem8.com/hc/en-us/articles/201190600-What-is-Track-My-Arrival)

### Inferences
- The customer never sees where the tech is *before* the on-my-way tap (no "3 stops away"), and loses the map at Start. The page is a travel-only artefact. TurnUp's "Booked in" → "On site" → "Paused" continuity is not something either product offers.
- Privacy is handled at the account level (admin toggle, which number is in the profile), not per visit; the tech cannot choose at send time whether to share location.
- Phone-based sharing (ServiceTitan, FieldPulse, ServiceM8) is the model closer to a one-van trade; the Force model assumes a fleet.

### Gaps

## Key question 8: Uber/DPD-style patterns adopted, and whether the trend is toward or away from promising minutes

### Takeaway
The field-service products have adopted the *look* of consumer tracking (live pin, photo, name, contact button) but not its *promises*: no stop-count, no "3 stops away", no route line for the customer, and ETA shown only where a verified-address GPS feed exists. The operational norm remains a window (Jobber 15 min–4 h options; practitioners citing 2 h) plus a tech-chosen minutes figure at departure, with enterprise suites adding "lock the communicated ETA". The direction is hybrid — window for the day, live ETA only during travel — rather than promising minutes up front.

### Cited Findings
- Consumer-app analogy is explicit in vendor marketing: customers "expect the same real-time visibility they get from food delivery apps and ride-sharing services" — [Fieldproxy (vendor blog)](https://www.fieldproxy.ai/blog/real-time-gps-tracking-customer-etas-building-trust-through-transparen-d1-32); Salesforce recommends updates with "real-time location and exact arrival time" to cut ETA calls — [Salesforce guide](https://www.salesforce.com/resources/guides/enhancing-customer-experience-in-field-service/)
- Live-pin features present: HCP (10 s refresh, Force), ServiceTitan (map + distance + ETA on verified address), FieldPulse (1 h live map), Jobber (US Force link) — see Q1/Q4 sources. None documents stop counts or queue position.
- Windows remain the scheduling primitive: Jobber offers 15 min to 4 h windows, after-start or centred — [Jobber Help](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/arrival-windows/); Elogii: "In most field operations, service time windows drive the entire scheduling process" — [Elogii](https://elogii.com/blog/customer-time-windows); ServiceTitan users describe 2 h windows with first-call set times and "approximate, will update if late" messaging — [ServiceTitan Community](https://community.servicetitan.com/t5/Call-Booking/How-are-you-scheduling-calls/m-p/11796)
- Enterprise suites bolt tracking onto windows: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Field Service day-of notification with estimated arrival and live tracking (2021 wave 1) — [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365-release-plan/2021wave1/service/dynamics365-field-service/modernize-customer-experience-technician-location-tracking-direct-customer-feedback); Oracle "communicated windows" with ETA locking — [Oracle docs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/field-service/fawmt/c-wmt-overview-use-communicated-windows.html); Comcast's tracker alerts ~30 min before arrival, reported as a trial — [CustomerThink](https://customerthink.com/?p=124465)
- Tech-chosen minutes, not computed: Jobber's 5–60 picker — [Jobber Help](https://help.getjobber.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021265374); FieldPulse's adjustable estimate — [FieldPulse Help](https://help.fieldpulse.com/en/articles/2517252-customer-communications-mobile-app)
- Link lifetimes are short and tied to travel: HCP 2 h / Start — [HCP Help](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/6060351-vehicle-gps-tracking-overview); FieldPulse 1 h — [FieldPulse Help](https://help.fieldpulse.com/en/articles/2517252-customer-communications-mobile-app); ServiceM8 only while Navigating — [ServiceM8 Help](https://support.servicem8.com/hc/en-us/articles/201190600-What-is-Track-My-Arrival)

### Inferences
- The products avoid *system-generated* minute promises except when they can compute them from a live feed and a verified destination; the human-chosen number in the on-my-way text is framed as the tech's own estimate, which shifts accountability to the person rather than the software. TurnUp's stage-based model (no minutes, just "On my way") is consistent with where the vendors have landed, and could add a computed ETA only under the same conditions ServiceTitan uses (verified address, live GPS).
- Stop-count / "you are next" indicators are absent across all six; for a one-van trade with a known day plan this is a cheap differentiator nobody in this set offers.

### Gaps
- No independent study comparing customer satisfaction for exact-time promises vs window-plus-tracking in home services; the "$37.7 billion wait-time cost" figure circulating in vendor content could not be traced to a primary source.
- Whether any of the six has shipped a stop-count or queue indicator since mid-2026: none found as of 10 Oct 2026.
