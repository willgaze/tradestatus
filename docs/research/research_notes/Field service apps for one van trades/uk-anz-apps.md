# UK and ANZ job management apps for small trade businesses (as of 10 Oct 2026)

Scope: Tradify, Commusoft, Fergus, Powered Now, YourTradebase, Workever, Joblogic, BigChange (JobWatch), Okappy, Gas Engineer Software (GES), Eworks Manager, plus Payaca, Klipboard and Trade2base (UK), with short notes on two ANZ reference points (ServiceM8 Track My Arrival, AroFlo SMS) because they are the closest analogues to a live job-tracking link. simPRO, Jobber, Housecall Pro and ServiceTitan are excluded (covered by another researcher).

Method note: vendor pricing and help-centre pages were fetched directly where possible; several Commusoft pages (pricing, "On the Way", vehicle tracking) returned 404 after a domain migration to commusoft.com/en-gb, so those findings rely on search-engine snapshots of the pages and are flagged. Review-site pages on Capterra sometimes blocked fetching (403); where so, findings come from search snippets of those pages.

---

## Key question 1: Core feature set, who it is for, UK pricing (GBP per user per month), free tiers

### Takeaway
For a one-van plumber the realistic UK price band is £18–£45 per user per month (GES £18–30, Powered Now £28–40, YourTradebase £29 flat, Workever £29–39, Klipboard £29, Tradify £34–44, Fergus £39–45 list, Joblogic from £45 annual). Commusoft (4-licence minimum, 12-month contract), Okappy (5–10 user minimums, paid yearly in advance), Eworks Manager (£130+/month per team), BigChange (quote only) and Payaca (£299/month, now aimed at solar/heat-pump installers) are not priced for a sole trader. Nobody in this set offers a permanent free tier except YourTradebase's limited post-trial free account.

### Inferences
- The sole-trader sweet spot is £18–£40/month, and the two UK-native products that explicitly price a single-user plan (Powered Now Business £28, Trade2base Starter £29, GES Starter £18) are the most direct competitors for a one-van plumber's wallet.
- Tradify's per-message SMS charging (£0.10) and Fergus's £8/100 SMS bundle set a UK market expectation that customer texts cost extra; a product bundling unlimited status messages would be noticed.
- Payaca, Commusoft, Okappy, BigChange and Eworks are effectively out of reach or out of scope for a sole trader on price structure alone.

### Gaps
- Commusoft's actual GBP per-licence price is not public; the new commusoft.com/en-gb pricing URL returned 404 at fetch time.
- BigChange's current price list is unpublished; all figures are third-party and may be years old.

## Key question 2: Customer-facing surfaces (confirmations, reminders, "on my way", live tracking, portals, quote/invoice acceptance and payment, review requests, two-way messaging, photos)

### Takeaway
Only three products in this set give the customer a live map link: Commusoft (SMS "On the Way" link with engineer photo, ETA and live map — but the map version depends on dedicated vehicle-tracking hardware), BigChange (ETA alert with "a link that opens a live map") and Joblogic (portal shows "engineer's current position while travelling", Enterprise-tier portal). Tradify, Fergus, YourTradebase, GES, Eworks and Workever have no customer-visible tracking at all; Tradify's "I'm on my way" is a pre-filled text that leaves from the plumber's own phone and is not even logged. Two-way SMS is rare (Fergus yes; Tradify explicitly one-way). No app in this set has native WhatsApp; review-request automation is only confirmed for Trade2base.

### Cited Findings

**Tradify**
- "I'm on my way!" option in the mobile app opens a message in the phone's own texting app; "The text message is sent from your phone and not from Tradify", so it does not appear in the Job Activity Log; ETA calculation needs GPS/location permission for Tradify — [Tradify ETA help article](https://help.tradifyhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/22417143010841-How-To-Send-an-ETA-Estimated-Time-of-Arrival-Text-Message)

### Inferences
- The "on my way" moment is handled by every app as a one-shot message; only Commusoft, BigChange and Joblogic turn it into something the customer can keep watching, and all three gate that behind enterprise-style pricing or hardware. A link that stays live through "On site", "Paused" and "Job done" has no direct equivalent in the UK sole-trader tier.
- Tradify's design (text from your own phone, not logged) means a dispute about whether the customer was told has no evidence trail; a product that logs the send and the customer's open would be a differentiator.
- Powered Now's "see who is visiting" and Joblogic's "engineer photo ID" show the market treats identity reassurance as part of the arrival message.
- Fergus's "Schedule not sent" red label is a cheap but effective UX pattern for the office side of confirmations.

### Gaps
- Commusoft's exact wording of the On the Way SMS and the price of the tracking-linked version were not found (pages 404 after domain move).
- Whether Joblogic's en-route/on-site customer notifications are available on Standard or need Premium/Enterprise is not stated.
- Workever's tier for conditional "on my way" SMS is unclear.
- No app in scope was confirmed to have native WhatsApp Business messaging.

## Key question 3: Arrival detection and time capture (GPS check-in, geofencing, automatic timesheets, mileage, reconstruction when offline)

### Takeaway
No app in this set automatically detects arrival for the customer-facing flow; every status change is a tap by the tradesperson. GPS is used for office visibility (Fergus employee map, Powered Now map, Tradify map, Joblogic/Commusoft vehicle tracking), and BigChange records arrival/departure timestamps when jobs are started/completed. Geofenced auto check-in was not found anywhere in scope. Timesheets are either a stopwatch (Tradify) or derived from status timestamps (Joblogic status audit, Commusoft travel/arrive/leave).

### Cited Findings
- Tradify: a built-in job timer "staff start on arrival" and stop on leaving; manual entry alternative; Map view shows staff and job locations and requires location permission; a settings toggle reduces tracking to save battery; no geofenced check-in documented — [Tradify timesheet feature page](https://www.tradifyhq.com/features/timesheet-software-app); [Tradify mobile guide](https://help.tradifyhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/5208081984281)
- Commusoft: the engineer taps Travel (fires the on-the-way SMS), Arrive (apply once inside the property; "Once marked as Arrived, the appointment cannot be edited or moved"), then Leave with a job-complete prompt; statuses applied in the app update the web schedule automatically; the article does not describe timestamps explicitly — [Commusoft appointment statuses](https://help.commusoft.com/en/articles/216882-appointment-statuses)
- Commusoft reviewer complaint: it "only logs travel when travelling to the job, not returning" — [Capterra Commusoft reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/149651/Commusoft/reviews/)
- Commusoft tracking uses dedicated hardware trackers rather than the phone — [Commusoft real-time vehicle tracking (search snapshot)](https://www.commusoft.co.uk/jobs/real-time-vehicle-tracking/)
- Fergus: GPS is a Professional-plan feature; staff enable a GPS toggle in the Fergus Go app with location set to "Always" on iPhone; "Fergus will only access GPS when the phone detects movement"; the employee map shows last known location, a daily timeline of pings, and "Orange arrows represent areas where reception was lost" — [Fergus setting up GPS tracking](https://help.fergus.com/en/articles/432916-setting-up-gps-tracking); [Fergus employee map](https://help.fergus.com/en/articles/425841-the-employee-map)
- Powered Now: Professional or Premium required; GPS plots the user on a map with appointments overlaid; "When the user starts moving their track is logged"; "Every point includes data time and speed data"; "Tracking will only work 'live' when there is an active data connection on your device"; tracking is "only enabled when the device is moving and active to save battery" — [Powered Now intro to location tracking](https://support.powerednow.com/en/knowledge/introduction-to-location-tracking); [Powered Now setting up location tracking](https://support.powerednow.com/en/knowledge/setting-up-location-tracking)

### Takeaway
Tradify is the notable outlier: its help centre says it does not work offline at all. ### Gaps

## Key question 5: Exceptions — late running, no-show (either side), customer not home, overruns, cancellations, customer-side rescheduling

### Takeaway
The enterprise-leaning UK products (Commusoft, Joblogic) have the most complete exception vocabulary: Commusoft's app offers Reject, Abort (at three points), No access (with mandatory photo) and Leave with a "Is the job complete?" prompt, and Joblogic adds Abandon, No Access (reason required, logs a revisit) and Leave Site (pauses the job). None of the sole-trader apps (Tradify, Powered Now, YourTradebase, GES) document a no-access or running-late workflow; Tradify relies on custom statuses (Pro) and texts from your own phone. Customer-side rescheduling exists only in Commusoft's rebook/cancel portal and, for booking rather than rescheduling, in Powered Now's and Workever's portals. No app documents a no-show fee or cancellation-fee mechanism.

### Cited Findings
- Commusoft statuses: Accept/Reject (reason) the night before or morning of; Travel (sends on-our-way SMS); Abort before travel, on arrival, or after work has started — each with a reason, "Only the appointment is aborted. The job stays open and can be rebooked"; "No access" with questions and a photo "to document the access issue"; Leave with "Is the job complete?" Yes/No; if marked Arrived in error, abort and reschedule; if leaving early for an emergency, abort or mark Left and reschedule — [Commusoft appointment statuses](https://help.commusoft.com/en/articles/216882-appointment-statuses)
- Commusoft advises that when a customer cancels you should abort rather than delete "to maintain an accurate historical record" — [Commusoft deleting a job](https://help.commusoft.com/en/articles/216767-deleting-a-job)
- Commusoft customers can rebook/cancel via the portal and book from a per-job link — [Commusoft customer portal](https://www.commusoft.com/en-gb/customer-portal/); [Commusoft booking portal help](https://help.commusoft.com/en/articles/714467-how-to-use-the-job-booking-portal-for-customers)
- Joblogic: Abort (travel or on-site stage, reason relayed to office); Abandon ("Logs a Revisit Required request"); No Access ("A Reason for the no access must be provided", logs the need for a revisit); Leave Site "Pauses the job and reverts it to 'Accepted'"; completion step "Resolved: No" sends the visit to "Requires revisit"; completion date/time can be back-dated with a warning; Abandon and No Access availability depends on permissions — [Joblogic mobile user guide](https://support.joblogic.com/docs/joblogic-mobile-user-guide)
- BigChange: ETA alerts exist partly to "reduce no-shows and failed not-at-home visits"; the app forces "Select a result" before a job can be closed — [BigChange ETA alerts](https://www.bigchange.com/features/eta-alerts); [BigChange JobWatch on App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/bigchange-jobwatch/id1281529950)
- Tradify: "Recurring Jobs & Custom Statuses" from Pro; employees cannot decline jobs and can be scheduled after leaving the company (4-star reviewer) — [Tradify global pricing](https://www.tradifyhq.com/pricing); [Trustpilot Tradify (UK)](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/tradifyhq.com)
- Powered Now: customers can book and see who is visiting via the Premium portal; the article does not mention rescheduling or cancelling — [Powered Now v9 customer portal](https://powerednow.com/powered-now-v9-introducing-our-new-customer-portal/)
- Workever: customers can "schedule appointments" in the portal — [Workever pricing](https://www.workever.com/pricing)
- Fergus: a quote-visit, job-event and overdue-invoice reminder set exists; no late/no-access flow documented — [Fergus send and receive SMS](https://help.fergus.com/en/articles/11174752-send-and-receive-sms)
- YourTradebase: no customer-side rescheduling described — [YourTradebase scheduling help](https://help.yourtradebase.com/article/47-schedule-appointments-and-work-dates)

### Inferences
- "Paused" as a first-class customer-visible state has a close internal analogue only in Joblogic's Leave Site; making it visible to the customer (with a reason such as "gone for parts, back by 14:00") would be new.
- The no-access photo requirement (Commusoft) and reason requirement (Joblogic) are the evidence patterns a sole trader would value if a customer later disputes a wasted trip; a product should timestamp, geotag and photograph "customer not home" in one tap and send the customer a message at the same moment.
- Absence of any documented late-running message (other than ad-hoc texts) across all apps is a gap TurnUp can fill with a "running late by N minutes" update that re-computes the ETA.

### Gaps
- No vendor documents no-show or cancellation fees, deposits or "late cancellation" policies within the app.
- Whether Commusoft's "No access" automatically notifies the customer was not found.
- Overrun handling (alerting the next customer) was not documented by any vendor.

## Key question 6: Job diary / timeline — what is logged automatically and who can see it

### Takeaway
Internal timelines are universal; what differs is whether customer messages are captured. Tradify logs Tradify-sent messages in a "Customer Communications History" (Pro) but not phone-sent on-my-way texts; Fergus logs SMS replies in Notes & History; Joblogic keeps a per-visit status audit with timestamps and lets engineers mark attachments private so they never reach customer jobsheets or portals; Powered Now stores customer replies on the customer timeline. Customer visibility of the timeline is limited to portal users (Commusoft, Joblogic Enterprise, Workever, Trade2base, Powered Now Premium).

### Cited Findings
- Tradify: Pro tier includes "Customer Communications History" (a log of communications sent from Tradify) and Plus includes "Staff Activity History"; the phone-sent on-my-way text "won't appear in Tradify's Job Activity Log" — [Tradify global pricing](https://www.tradifyhq.com/pricing); [Tradify ETA help article](https://help.tradifyhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/22417143010841-How-To-Send-an-ETA-Estimated-Time-of-Arrival-Text-Message)
- Fergus: a searchable SMS log with timestamps and message content; replies "appear in that job's Notes & History" — [Fergus send and receive SMS](https://help.fergus.com/en/articles/11174752-send-and-receive-sms); [Fergus SMS how-to](https://fergus.com/tradehub/how-to-videos/sms/)
- Joblogic: "Visit status history" with time and date stamps; status audit in completion flow; "Make Private" keeps attachments off customer jobsheets and portals; "Notify Office" flags notes to the office — [Joblogic mobile user guide](https://support.joblogic.com/docs/joblogic-mobile-user-guide); "Office and field staff receive real-time updates" — [Joblogic Standard product page](https://www.joblogic.com/products/standard/)
- Commusoft: statuses applied in the app update the web schedule automatically — [Commusoft appointment statuses](https://help.commusoft.com/en/articles/216882-appointment-statuses)
- Powered Now: customer replies to communications "are stored on the customer's timeline" — [Powered Now v9 customer portal](https://powerednow.com/powered-now-v9-introducing-our-new-customer-portal/)
- Okappy: "Traceable job history" listed — [Okappy features](https://www.okappy.com/features/)
- Fergus GPS: daily timeline of pings per employee, visible to office — [Fergus employee map](https://help.fergus.com/en/articles/425841-the-employee-map)

### Inferences
- A sole trader has no office to read the diary, so the useful audience for a timeline is the customer (as reassurance) and the tradesperson's future self (as dispute evidence). Only portal-based products expose it to customers, and portals need logins or at least links.

### Gaps
- No vendor documents retention periods for status or GPS history.
- Who can see Commusoft's status log beyond office staff was not found.
